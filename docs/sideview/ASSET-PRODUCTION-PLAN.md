# Remaining art production plan

The prototype demonstrates the requested production-method and perspective change. It does **not** establish parity with all four references, and it is not permission to convert the rest of the district.

1. **Protagonist animation is the first acceptance risk.** The image-assisted sheet contains six frames, but some contact/passing phases repeat. A human pixel animator should repaint a complete six- or eight-phase walk at the current 88×96 cell size (80px visual height, 90px foot baseline), alternating near/far legs and opposite arm swings. Keep face volume, satchel registration and shoulder height stable; use two carefully chosen vertical bob pixels. Retain blink/breathing idle poses, and paint a proper hop/landing and tea-holding pose. Do not merely interpolate duplicated frames.
2. **Vehicle timing/registration.** Preserve the detailed original rickshaw/CNG silhouettes, but repaint axle-registered wheel rotation and the driver's pedal/leg cycle. The current three-frame draft is limited. Inspect body stability in motion, not just alpha boxes or frame hashes. Add a subtle CNG engine/exhaust loop with no drifting body panels.
3. **Pixel-cluster cleanup.** Use the normalized PNGs as editable source drafts. Simplify the protagonist's face/hair/folds to intentional clusters; remove stray coloured edge pixels. Resolve dense, noisy facade/foliage areas so large-value shapes read first. Review at native 640×360 and integer 2×, without blur or a filter disguising poor clusters.
4. **Family consistency.** Unify the driver, seller, pedestrian and protagonist's outline weight, head/body exaggeration and material highlights. The driver is currently more naturally proportioned than the protagonist. Keep background texture density subordinate to the protagonist.
5. **Lighting and parallax polish.** Keep the current blue/peach sky, indigo shadows, amber shop lamps and four depth speeds. Hand-check layer silhouettes for cutout artefacts and repetitive tile edges. Add a small dedicated cloud/boat layer only after the focal street is approved.
6. **Mobile/native UI review.** Retain the native-size Bengali text layer and crisp English HUD. Review portrait spacing, dialogue wrapping, touch areas and moving sign alignment on physical Android/iOS devices. Automated mobile testing is Chromium emulation.
7. **Only after visual approval:** finalize the palette/style bible, publish the approved sprite/animation manifest, then adapt the remaining district scenes. Revision 0.2 stays on its original branch and behind `?view=district`; do not merge the prototype to main or silently migrate its world coordinates.

Human Bengali voice recordings remain an independent missing asset. No paid service or purchase was used or authorized by this implementation. This plan identifies work still needed; it is not a claim that a professional pixel animator has completed it.
